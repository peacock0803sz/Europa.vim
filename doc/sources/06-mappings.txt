MAPPINGS						*europa-mappings*

Europa.vim does not define any default key mappings in Phase 2.

The viewer buffer is read-only (`modifiable=false`), so normal text-editing
keys do nothing harmful. Navigation (`j`/`k`, `Ctrl-D`/`Ctrl-U`, etc.)
works as in any other buffer.

PLUG MAPPINGS (PHASE 3+)~
						*europa-mappings-plug*
`<Plug>(europa-*)` mapping handles will be introduced in Phase 3 alongside
the editing and execution commands. Setting `g:europa_use_default_mappings`
to `v:true` is a no-op in Phase 2 and is reserved for future use.
